    API Reference

    This page is a compact map of the current PulseLib public API. Classes marked internal in source are omitted unless they explain a public workflow.

    Animatables and controllers¶

    • PAnimatable<T> and PItemAnimatable<T> define animatable objects.
    • PAnimationManager<T> owns regular and graph controller factories, live controllers, and the bound model.
    • PAnimationController<T> plays a PRawAnimation or PAnimationGraph, exposes graph parameters, controls timing, and owns persistent Molang state.
    • InstanceAnimationManager<T>, SingletonAnimationManager<T>, and AnimManagerKey provide per-instance and keyed state.
    • ControllerState is PLAY, PAUSE, or STOP.

    Animation data and graphs¶

    • PRawAnimation is a builder for staged playback sequences; PAnimationType selects once, hold, or cycle behavior.
    • PAnimationGraph, PAnimationState, PAnimationTransition, PCondition, and PAnimationGraphRuntime implement graph states, blend spaces, overlays, transitions, and runtime state.
    • PAnimationParameters stores graph numeric/boolean inputs and one-use triggers. PInterruptionPolicy is used by graph transitions; PTransitionInterruptionPolicy is used by player-animation activation crossfades.
    • PAnimation, PBoneAnimation, PAnimationTrack<T>, and PKeyframe<T> represent parsed animation data.
    • PAnimationChannelType<T> is the generic channel contract. PAnimationChannel supplies vector and quaternion implementations; PulseLib registers position, rotation, and scale channel types.
    • PInterpolation and PInterpolationType control keyframe interpolation. PBlendMode and PPoseBlendMode control pose blending.
    • PPose, PModelPose, and PAnimationPoseResolver evaluate local and model-space poses.
    • PRootMotionRuntime and PRootMotionDelta extract incremental root motion from an animation.
    • PAnimationEvent and PAnimationEventDispatcher provide animation sound and particle events.

    Models and loaders¶

    • PModelData and its DefaultBlockModelData, DefaultItemModelData, DefaultEntityModelData, and DefaultEntityLayerModelData builders identify models and textures.
    • PModel, PBone, and PMesh are raw data. PBakedModel, PBakedBone, and PBakedMesh are the baked runtime representation.
    • PMeshRenderContext and PMeshRenderResolver customize render type, colour, lighting, overlay, and deformation for each baked mesh.
    • PModelCache manages loaders, resource reloads, baking, and cache cleanup. PModelLoader is the extension point.
    • glTF classes are in data.gltf: PGltfModelLoader, PGltfModelParser, and PGltfAnimationEventSidecarParser.
    • Gecko and Molang classes are in data.gecko: PGeckoModelLoader, PGeckoModelParser, PGeckoAnimationEventParser, and MolangParser.

    Rendering, textures, and attachments¶

    • PRenderer<T, RS>, PBlockRenderer<T, RS>, PItemRenderer<T, RS>, PEntityRenderer<T, RS>, and PEntityRenderLayer<T, RS> are renderer entry points.
    • PRenderTypes provides compatible triangle render types; PRenderQueue batches their submissions.
    • PTextureCache, RuntimeLoader, and PLibSpriteMetadata manage the runtime texture atlas and emissive metadata.
    • Attachments are centered on PLivingAttachmentDefinition, PLivingAttachments, PAttachmentBinding, and PAttachmentAnchor.

    Player animations¶

    • PPlayerAnimationDefinition configures bindings, masks, blend modes, weights, activation crossfades, cycle synchronization, controllers, Molang queries, and animation-owned mesh deformers through Builder.deform(...).
    • PPlayerAnimations, PPlayerAnimationHandle, and PPlayerAnimationInstance expose runtime registration and per-player state.
    • PPlayerPart, PPlayerAnimationMask, PPlayerAnimationBlendMode, and PPlayerAnimationWeight define how a player pose is applied.
    • PPlayerAnimationDeformer, PPlayerAnimationDeformerValueSource, and PPlayerAnimationDeformerContext attach a deformer stack to a definition and sample channels from its player/controller context.

    Mesh deformers¶

    • PMeshDeformer, PDeformerInstance, PDeformerStack, and PChannelReference define and evaluate reusable deformations.
    • GPU-supported built-in operations are PBendDeformer, PHingeDeformer, PTwistDeformer, PStretchDeformer, PSquashDeformer, PTaperDeformer, and PWaveDeformer.
    • PMeshDeformation selects optional subdivision and carries runtime values. PGpuDeformerBuffers uploads supported stacks for queued and instant shaders; PDeformedMeshBuffers is the fallback cache. PPlayerMeshDeformers is the independent player-binding API.

    Events and helpers¶

    • PulseLibEvents exposes texture, attachment, and player-animation registration events.
    • PLibHelper, PLibCodecs, and PLibDatabase are common integration helpers.
